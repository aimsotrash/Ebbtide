# Hybrid retrieval: keyword search fused with the embeddings

Generated by `python -m bench.hybrid` on 2026-10-31 (about 1 minute on a laptop CPU). Its rules, including when the server would switch to hybrid ranking, are in the docstring of [`bench/hybrid.py`](hybrid.py) and were fixed before the first run.
